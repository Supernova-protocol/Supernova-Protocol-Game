Privacy Policy

NOVA CORE: Protocol 51 · last updated October 10, 2026

This page explains what NOVA CORE stores about you and why. Short version: we only keep what is needed to show a fair leaderboard and to contact giveaway winners.

When you just play

Your callsign, settings and upgrades are saved only in your own browser (local storage). Nothing is sent to us.

When you sign in with X

Signing in with X is optional. It is only needed for your scores to count on the global leaderboard and for giveaways. X then shares with us:

  • your X username (@handle), display name and profile picture
  • your X account ID (a number that identifies your account)
  • the email address on your X account, if X provides one

For each ranked run we also store your callsign, score, level, kills, and when the run started and ended.

Why we use it

  • To show the leaderboard and prove which X account set each score.
  • To spot and remove fake or cheated scores.
  • To contact giveaway winners via X.

We never post on X for you, never sell your data and never use it for ads.

What is public

On the leaderboard everyone can see your callsign, your @handle, your score and your level. Your email address is never shown.

Where it is stored

Leaderboard data and sign-in accounts are stored with our database provider, Supabase. The game itself is hosted on Vercel.

Deleting your data

To have your account and all your scores deleted, message @SupernovaLPad. We delete them within 30 days.
