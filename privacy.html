Privacy Policy

NOVA CORE: Protocol 51 · last updated 11 October 2026

This page explains what NOVA CORE stores about you and why. Short version: we only keep what is needed to show a fair leaderboard and to contact giveaway winners.

When you just play

Your callsign, settings (such as language, sound and aim assist), progress and upgrades are saved only in your own browser (local storage). Nothing is sent to us.

When you sign in with Google

Signing in with Google is optional. It is only needed for your scores to count on the global leaderboard and for giveaways. Google then shares with us:

  • your name and profile picture
  • your email address
  • your Google account ID (a number that identifies your account)

For each ranked run we also store your callsign, score, level, kills, and when the run started and ended.

Why we use it

  • To show the leaderboard and prove which account set each score.
  • To spot and remove fake or cheated scores.
  • To contact giveaway winners by email.

We never post anything for you, never sell your data and never use it for ads.

What is public

On the leaderboard everyone can see your callsign, a short player tag (like #7F3A), your score and your level. Your name and email address are never shown.

Where it is stored

Leaderboard data and sign-in accounts are stored with our database provider, Supabase. The game itself is hosted on Vercel.

Deleting your data

To have your account and all your scores deleted, message @SupernovaLPad on X. We delete them within 30 days.
